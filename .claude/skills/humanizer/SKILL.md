# Humanizer

Strip the tells that mark text as AI-generated while preserving every fact and claim exactly.

## What to eliminate

- **Em dashes (—)**: replace with a period, comma, colon, or parentheses depending on the rhythm — never just delete the connective meaning.
- **Overused AI vocabulary**: delve, leverage, robust, seamless, furthermore, moreover, "in today's world/landscape", unlock, elevate, game-changer, "boils down to", "it's important to note", "in conclusion", "navigate the complexities/landscape of", "testament to", "dive into", "unpack".
- **Reflexive triplets**: "X, Y, and Z" used as a default list structure everywhere — vary sentence and list length instead.
- **False balance hedging**: "on one hand... on the other hand..." when a human would just take a position.
- **Uniform sentence length**: human writing mixes short, punchy sentences with longer ones — break up monotone rhythm.
- **Excessive hedging**: "might potentially", "could arguably", stacked qualifiers.
- **Over-formatting**: bolding every other phrase, excessive bullet points where prose reads better.

## Process

1. Rewrite preserving the original meaning and every fact exactly — this is a register/rhythm/word-choice pass, not a content rewrite. Never add new claims or drop true ones while humanizing.
2. Vary sentence length and structure deliberately; don't just swap individual words.
3. Read the result as if spoken aloud — if a person wouldn't actually say it that way, revise again.
4. Do a final pass specifically hunting for em dashes and the vocabulary list above — these are the highest-signal AI tells and are easy to miss on a single read-through.
