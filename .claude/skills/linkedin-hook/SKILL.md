# LinkedIn Hook

LinkedIn truncates posts after roughly 2-3 lines before showing "see more" — the hook has to work standalone, before any context has loaded.

## Process

1. If the angle or tone isn't already specified, ask briefly (inline, not a full interview): what's the post about, and which direction — punchy/contrarian, curiosity-gap (open a tension without resolving it), data-led (a specific surprising number), story-led, or question-led. Also confirm the audience if it's unclear.
2. Hook techniques to draw on:
   - Contrarian claim ("most people think X — they're wrong")
   - Specific, surprising number or result
   - Blunt confession or mistake
   - Pattern interrupt: a short fragment, not a full sentence
   - A direct question the reader actually wants answered (not a rhetorical throwaway)
3. Avoid generic openers: "I'm excited to announce", "In today's fast-paced world", "Let me tell you a story about", questions with obvious answers.
4. Keep the hook to 1-2 short lines. No em dashes, no AI-tell vocabulary (delve, leverage, robust, unlock, game-changer, etc. — same list as the humanizer skill).
5. Provide 3 hook variants per request so the user can pick, unless they ask for just one.
