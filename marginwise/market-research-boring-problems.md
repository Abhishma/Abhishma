# Marginwise market research: boring problems we can solve

Australia, New Zealand (Auckland), US, Canada, Germany (Berlin), Saudi Arabia. Research date 17 Oct 2026.

How to read this: "boring problems" are operational, recurring and often compliance-driven. Brands must fix them whether or not the economy is good, which makes them easier to sell than "growth". Every statistic has a source in section 12. Anything marked (unverified) has not been confirmed against a primary source and must not go into client-facing material until it is. Deal sizes and forecasts are our planning estimates, not market data.

Planning exchange rates used for USD pricing (check before quoting): 1 AUD = 0.66 USD, 1 NZD = 0.59 USD, 1 CAD = 0.72 USD, 1 EUR = 1.10 USD, 1 USD = 3.75 SAR (pegged).

## 0. The decision in ten lines

1. Start with **Australia**, and run the **US** alongside it. Both are English-speaking, the Instagram account already targets them, and each has a live trigger right now: the Australian card surcharge ban (1 Oct 2026) and US tariff repricing.
2. Add **New Zealand** at almost no extra cost. Sell to it through Xero bookkeepers and trans-Tasman brands, using the same AU content.
3. Start **Germany (Berlin)** in month 2, aimed at English-speaking startups. Lead with B2B e-invoicing (mandatory from 1 Jan 2027) and the withdrawal-button and accessibility rules. Before you start, line up a German-speaking reviewer and a law-firm partner.
4. Start **Canada** in month 2 as an extension of the US work. Lead with cross-border duties and Quebec French requirements.
5. Start **Saudi Arabia** in month 3, through white-label partners only. Lead with ZATCA Wave 25 e-invoicing (deadline 1 Feb 2027). Do not sell brand marketing there yet.
6. Lead with **fixed-fee entry offers** ($1,500 to $8,000). Then convert clients into retainers: performance marketing, CRO, and AI agent monitoring. The retainers are where the money compounds.
7. The edge to sell: **integration plus AI at India cost**. Most local agencies do creative work or platform work. Few do Shopify plus ERP plus Salesforce plus compliance in a single fixed scope.
8. Price at about 40-60% below local agencies. Sell the outcome, not the hourly rate. Buyers read a very low price as risk.
9. 90-day booked revenue forecast across all markets: **$11.5K conservative, $72K base, $162K stretch** (section 7). The base case assumes about 1,500 targeted contacts and an average deal size of $5-8K.
10. Biggest risk: delivery capacity and trust. Fix both with a named founder on calls, a fixed-scope contract, and one case study from a demo store before day 21.

**Why not start with Germany, even though the money is good:**
- German customer copy and legal text need native German review.
- Cold email to businesses without consent is generally unlawful there (section 9).
- Compliance work needs a law-firm partner to sign off.

Set these up during wave 1 so wave 2 starts ready.

**Why Saudi Arabia goes last:**
- Buying is driven by relationships.
- Arabic is needed for copy and calls.
- Government and enterprise work needs a local entity.

The ZATCA deadline is real, but the cheap way to reach it is through partners.

**The UK** was not part of this research. It stays in the Instagram mix and should be revisited at day 90.

## 4. Market deep dives

### 4.1 Australia

**Snapshot**
- Online spend: A$82.6bn in 2025, up about 14%, around 24% of retail (Australia Post 2026 report).
- Shopify stores: about 152K active (Storeleads, May 2026, secondary). About 4,169 Shopify Plus sites (BuiltWith).
- Labour cost: minimum wage A$26.44/hr from 1 Jul 2026, which strengthens the case for automation.
- AI search: 83% of Australians have used an AI assistant for shopping research (vendor survey, Aug 2026).

**Forced-demand calendar**

| Date | Change | Who must act |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Oct 2026 (live) | RBA ban on surcharging Visa, Mastercard and eftpos cards, plus lower interchange | Every checkout that surcharged |
| 10 Dec 2026 | Privacy Act: privacy policies must describe automated decision-making | Brands using AI personalisation or fraud scoring |
| 1 Apr 2027 | Foreign-card interchange cap and transparency rules | Payments |
| 1 Jul 2027 | Unfair trading practices, subscription traps and drip pricing ban (penalties up to A$100M) | Subscription brands, any checkout that adds fees late |
| Pending | Privacy tranche 2 (exposure draft Aug 2026) | CRM and marketing data |
| Live now (market shock) | Sendle shut down Jan 2026; Australia Post fuel surcharge rose to 12% | Shopify SMB shipping |
| Live now (platform) | Shopify Scripts stopped running 30 Jun 2026; Magento 2.4.6 support ended Aug 2026 | Plus and Magento merchants |

**Boring problems by stack**
- **Shopify D2C:**
  - Surcharge removal and repricing.
  - Shipping cost hidden until checkout.
  - Afterpay/Zip checkout clutter.
  - Carrier rewiring after Sendle.
  - Payout reconciliation in Xero/MYOB.
  - Scripts-to-Functions clean-up.
  - Returns (41% of online shoppers returned an item).
  - Subscription cancel flows.
- **Salesforce:**
  - Sales Cloud data hygiene and lead routing for A$10-200M B2B distributors.
  - Service Cloud deflection: Fisher & Paykel reports 30% fewer calls with Agentforce (vendor-reported).
- **Custom / legacy:**
  - Cin7, Unleashed, MYOB or NetSuite sync failures (documented in app reviews and vendor FAQs).
  - Magento end-of-life replatforming.
  - B2B ordering by email or PDF.
  - Peppol for suppliers to the Commonwealth government (target, not a mandate).

**Buyer signals**
- Surcharge text in checkout ("1.5% card fee").
- Cin7 or Unleashed apps with 1-2 star reviews.
- Seek job ads for "ecommerce operations coordinator" or "Salesforce administrator".
- Flat-rate-only shipping.
- Old Magento version strings.
- Recharge or Skio installed.

**Working hours:** An ANZ shift of 06:30-15:30 IST gives about 5 hours of overlap with Sydney in summer, and 1 hour more in the southern winter.

- Auckland is about 38% of NZ's economy, with about 222K business units.
- Auckland GDP softened in the year to March 2025, so buyers are cost-sensitive and want ROI-framed automation.
- Minimum wage is NZ$23.95/hr from 1 Apr 2026.

**Forced-demand calendar**

| Date | Change | Who must act |

| 1 Jan 2027 | Peppol e-invoicing to government required for suppliers with NZ$33M+ revenue | B2B suppliers |
| Ongoing | Commerce Commission scrutiny of subscription traps | Subscription brands |

**Boring problems by stack**

- **Salesforce:** low relevance at this size.

**Fit:** Deal sizes are about 20-30% below Australia (estimate). Treat NZ as a partner-led add-on to Australia: sell through Xero bookkeepers and through trans-Tasman brands that already sell in AU.

**Working hours:** Auckland in summer is 7.5 hours ahead of IST. The best overlap is 2-3 hours, at 06:30-09:30 IST.

### 4.5 Germany (Berlin)

**Snapshot**
- 2025 online sales: EUR 92.3bn net, up 3.9% (HDE). 2026 forecast: up 4.3%. 57% of online sales go through marketplaces.
- Platforms:
  - Shopware leads among the top 1,000 shops with 11.5% (EHI 2025).
  - Shopify leads among SMB shops at about 40% (secondary).
  - About 121K live Shopify stores and about 20K Shopware stores; Shopware is down 11% year on year.
- Berlin startups: 218 funding rounds and EUR 2.68bn raised in 2025. 42% of Berlin startup employees are international, which makes English-first selling viable.
- IT skills shortage: about 79K IT roles unfilled (Bitkom, Sep 2026).

**Forced-demand calendar**

| Date | Change | Who must act |
|---|---|---|

| 19 Jun 2026 (live) | Withdrawal button (new section 356a of the civil code, BGB) | Every B2C shop |
| 2 Aug 2026 (live) | EU AI Act Art. 50: chatbots must disclose that they are AI | Every AI chatbot we build |

| 1 Jan 2028 | E-invoicing for all businesses | Remaining B2B |

**Boring problems by stack**
- **Shopify and Shopware:**
  - Exposure to warning letters (Abmahnungen): 18% of retailers received one in 2024 (Händlerbund).
  - Accessibility, withdrawal button, strike-through prices, product safety data, green claims, consent.
  - Payment mix: 2025 checkout share was PayPal 28.7%, invoice purchase (Kauf auf Rechnung) 26.1%, direct debit 14.4%, cards 13.7% (EHI).
  - Fashion returns.
  - German-language AI search: AI Overviews cut clicks by 17.8% in one German study.
- **Salesforce:** GDPR-compliant CRM with a consent model and deletion routines, Agentforce readiness for Mittelstand B2B.
- **Custom:**
  - Shopware 5 end of life (Jul 2024).
  - Magento 2.4.6 end of support.
  - JTL, plentymarkets, Xentral or SAP sync.
  - Multi-country EU expansion: OSS VAT, hreflang, translation.

**Buyer signals**
- No withdrawal button in the footer or account area.
- Overlay accessibility widgets.
- Shopware 5 paths in page source.
- "Klimaneutral" in product page copy.
- A B2B or trader ("Händler") login on a Shopify store.
- Tax advisers (Steuerberater) posting about the e-invoicing deadline.

**Language:** Berlin startups are fine in English. The Mittelstand needs German for sales and contracts. Make native German review a standard, priced line item on every project.

**Data protection:** India has no EU adequacy decision, so every engagement needs a GDPR processing agreement (AVV, Art. 28), Standard Contractual Clauses and a transfer impact assessment.

**Working hours:** 10:00-19:00 IST covers 09:00-14:30 CET in winter. This is the best overlap of any market in this research.

### 4B.6 How to build the lead list legally

- **Google Maps / Places API:**
  - Do not scrape or store Maps data in a CRM.
  - You may store only the place_id. Latitude and longitude may be cached for up to 30 days.
  - Use the API only for live checks.
- **Use official registers instead:**

  | Market | Registers |
  |---|---|
  | AU | ABN Lookup and bulk extract, NSW Fair Trading, QBCC, VBA, WA Building and Energy, AHPRA, Tax Practitioners Board, NDIS register |
  | NZ | NZBN API (has industry codes), PGDB, EWRB |
  | US | State licence boards (Texas TDLR, Arizona ROC, Florida DBPR, California CSLB), state bar and dental boards |
  | Canada | RBQ, CMMTQ, TSSA, Technical Safety BC |
  | Germany | Handwerkskammer Berlin database, trade guild lists (Innungen), installer locators, KZV dentist search, StBK directory. Collect in small volumes and record your lawful basis |
  | KSA | Saudi Business Center CR lookup, REGA broker register, Contractors Authority register, Yellow Pages KSA |

- **Qualifying signals to score each lead on:**
  - Fewer than 30 reviews, a rating under 4.5, or unanswered reviews.
  - No website, a slow one, or a broken one.
  - No online booking and no prices shown.
  - Voicemail on an after-hours test call.
  - Advertising on hipages, Angi, Thumbtack, Builderscrack or LSA (they already pay for leads).
  - Hiring a receptionist or admin.
  - 5 or more staff.
  - No WhatsApp button (KSA).
  - Likely above the EUR 800K turnover line (E-Rechnung) or SAR 187.5K (ZATCA).
- **Outreach law:**
  - US: SMS needs A2P 10DLC registration and TCPA consent for marketing texts.
  - Canada: CASL applies. Scraped lists do not count as consent.
  - Germany: no cold email or cold calls to businesses without consent.
  - Australia and NZ: Spam Act and UEMA rules, plus the Do Not Call Register for calls.
  - KSA: PDPL consent rules apply.

**Outreach law to follow (not legal advice):**
- **Australia:** Spam Act 2003 allows "inferred consent" only for a conspicuously published business address where the message relates to the person's role. Identify the sender and include a working unsubscribe.
- **New Zealand:** Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007; same approach as Australia.
- **US:** CAN-SPAM requires a physical address, an opt-out honoured within 10 business days, and honest subject lines.
- **Canada:** CASL allows implied consent only for conspicuously published addresses relevant to the role, with no "no unsolicited messages" notice. Identify the sender, give an address and include an unsubscribe. Penalties are high.
- **Germany:** UWG section 7 generally prohibits cold advertising email to businesses without prior consent. **Do not cold email German contacts.** Use LinkedIn conversations, partner introductions and inbound leads, and email only after someone asks for it.
- **Saudi Arabia:** use LinkedIn and partner introductions. Use WhatsApp only after the person opts in.

### 11.5 Testing and kill rules (our heuristics)

- **Kill an ad** when either is true:
  - it has spent 2x the target cost per lead with zero leads, or
  - its 3-second hook rate is below 20% after 1,000 impressions.
- **Kill a concept** after 7 days if its cost per DM keyword is more than 2x the account median.
- **Scale a winner** by 20-30% every 3-4 days, and copy it into retargeting.
- **Frequency:**
  - Cold audiences: under 2.5 a week.
  - Retargeting: 4-6 a week while the audience is small.
  - Refresh creative when CTR falls 30% from its peak.
- **Metrics, in order:**
  1. Hook rate.
  2. Cost per DM keyword.
  3. DM-to-email capture.
  4. Cost per lead.
  5. **Cost per booked call.**
  6. Show rate.
  7. Audit close rate.
  8. CAC per paid audit.

### 11.7 Ad compliance

- **Germany and EU:**
  - DSA beneficiary and payer fields (ads will not publish without them).
  - Privacy policy on every form.
  - Unticked consent box for marketing email.
  - Data processing agreement with ManyChat.
  - German privacy notice.
  - Expect weaker signals because of Meta's "less personalised ads" option.
- **Canada (CASL):** an inquiry gives implied consent for 6 months. Ask for express consent in the DM or form, and identify the sender and include an unsubscribe in every email.
- **Australia (Spam Act):** consent required, sender identified, unsubscribe honoured within 5 working days.
- **Saudi Arabia:** check with local advice whether the Mawthooq advertising licence applies to a foreign B2B advertiser (unverified). Run Arabic and English creative with conservative imagery.
- **Meta "Unrealistic outcomes" policy:**
  - No "add $200K profit" claims.
  - No "guaranteed compliant".
  - No personal-attribute phrasing ("Are you losing money?"). Write "Shopify brands lose..." instead.
  - Say "readiness checklist", not legal advice.

### 11.8 Five mistakes to avoid

1. Many small interest or lookalike ad sets on $50 a day. Consolidate instead.
2. Near-duplicate creatives. Andromeda treats them as one ad.
3. Optimising for cheap DMs instead of booked calls. Send CRM events back.
4. IG DM ads into Germany, missing DSA fields, or over-promising claims.
5. Launching all six markets at once, so each gets $8 a day and none learns anything.

**Verify before launch:**
- Which detailed-targeting options still exist (search in Ads Manager).
- Whether the Higher-intent form supports a GDPR consent box.
- Whether Mawthooq applies.
- KSA CPM.

## 12. What could go wrong

| Risk | Likelihood | Effect | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Founder time is too thin for 1,500 contacts plus delivery | High | Forecast slips to conservative | Hire one SDR or VA for list building from week 2. Templates plus personalisation fields. Delivery pod from week 4 |
| "Offshore" trust objection | High | Low close rate | Founder on every call in local hours. Fixed scope with acceptance criteria. 50% upfront, not 100%. Demo-store case study. Local entity later (US LLC first) |
| Compliance work treated as legal advice | Medium | Liability | "We implement, your counsel signs off" in every proposal. Law-firm partner in DE, AU, CA. Professional indemnity insurance before the first compliance project |
| Cold email breaks local law (Germany, Canada) | Medium in CA, high in DE | Fines, reputation | No cold email in Germany. CASL-compliant lists only in Canada. Separate sending domain |
| A deadline moves or a rule is changed | Medium | Offer loses urgency | Re-check each rule's status before a campaign; stats marked (unverified) stay out of client copy |
| Too many markets at once | High | Nothing lands | Wave gates (section 2). Do not start wave 2 until the gate is met |
| AI agents misfire in production (wrong price, wrong invoice) | Medium | Client loss | Human approval on any price or invoice action. Logs. EU AI Act disclosure on chatbots |
| Currency and payment friction | Low | Slow cash | Wise or Stripe in USD, AUD, EUR, CAD. Invoice in the client's currency |
| HIRE Act (US offshore tax) passes | Low (unverified) | US price advantage shrinks | Watch. Price on outcomes. Consider a US entity |
