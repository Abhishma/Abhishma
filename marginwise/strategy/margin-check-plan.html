Marginwise lead magnet, 30 Oct 2026

From store check to Margin Check

What the free store check misses from the user's side, who it has to serve, and how it becomes the main source of leads.

Inputs: the check as built (Lovable commit 0c3ee32), 6 competing free audit tools, Shopify Community merchant feedback (Aug-Oct 2026), and the 7 personas from the growth plan. Competitor claims are their own; targets are assumptions.

The finding

A free home-page audit is no longer a lead magnet on its own. At least six free Shopify audit tools launched in 2026, most with no signup, and most check more than ours. Ours checks one page, scores every visitor the same way and never mentions money, even though the brand promise is profit-first. Merchants want 3 to 5 ranked fixes with evidence, likely impact and who has to do the fix. The opening: a Margin Check that answers "what is this costing me, in my currency" and adapts to who you are and where you sell.

What merchants ask for

  • 3 to 5 ranked fixes with likely impact, not a bare score
  • Evidence for each finding and a plain "why it matters"
  • No false positives; say "couldn't tell" instead of guessing
  • "You can fix this" versus "needs a developer"
  • Re-check after fixing to see what improved

What agencies learn

Blanket "free audit" offers get ignored. What works is one specific observation that proves you looked at their business (r/agency).

The Margin Check

"See what your store is costing you, in your currency, in 60 seconds. No signup."

  1. InputsStore URL, one tap on "I'm a...", market from location, optional revenue band to turn findings into money.
  2. CrawlHome, one product page, cart, shipping and returns policies, robots.txt, sitemap, llms.txt. Speed from Google PageSpeed (real mobile LCP, CLS, INP).
  3. Money firstA range in local currency from sourced ratios applied to their own revenue band, labelled "planning scenario". No band, no money figure.
  4. Each findingEvidence, confidence (measured, likely, couldn't tell), why it matters, who fixes it. Ordered by persona and market.
  5. Keep itOptional: email me this report and re-check in 30 days. The main lead capture, offered after value is shown.
  6. Next step by personaThe teardown request carries the check result, so the team starts with context.

Persona modules, the ops leak quiz, one-problem search pages, anonymous totals for the data story.

What could go wrong

False positives

"Couldn't tell" over guessing, saved HTML fixtures for every check, weekly review of 10 random reports.

Money read as a promise

Always a range, always sourced, always "planning scenario", only from the visitor's own revenue band.

Crawling others' stores

Public pages only, a few requests per check, honest user agent, robots.txt respected, rate limit kept.

Privacy

No store data kept without consent; totals only in aggregate; consent wording for the Privacy Act, UK GDPR and DPDP.

Team time

Teardowns only for persona-fit stores above a revenue band; everyone else gets the self-fix report.

Costs

PageSpeed API is free within quota. Live AI-answer checks cost per query, so they stay in the paid AI Visibility Snapshot.
