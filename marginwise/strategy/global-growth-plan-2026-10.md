# Marginwise global growth plan: one desk for every store problem (28 Oct 2026)

Built from four research passes:
- 59 competitor agencies across Australia, the UK, the US and India
- a global benchmark of how agencies package and price each service, including single-point-of-contact models (25+ agencies)
- lead-generation evidence and named opportunities by region
- 7 evidence-based buyer personas

Competitor prices and results quoted here are the agencies' own claims.

## 1. The decision in one paragraph
Become the one desk a growing Shopify brand calls for anything that touches revenue or margin: ads, conversion, the store and its integrations, search and AI visibility, and the boring operations AI can take over. Sell it as **Marginwise Desk**, a monthly plan with a named lead, one channel and one request board, at published local prices. Use small fixed-price entry offers to start the relationship. Win the first clients through referral partners and trigger-based outreach in Australia. Report every month on contribution profit, not ROAS.

## 2. Why this position is open
- **Nobody publishes prices.** Only 4 of 59 agencies show any price; none of the 10 Australian agencies reviewed does.
- **Nobody sells AI for operations as a product.** Competitor "AI" means creative, reporting or "agentic readiness". No one sells order-status, ERP sync, payout reconciliation or phone agents as packaged services.
- **Single-point-of-contact models exist only at the top end or the bottom end.** Gild Group (US) runs fractional ecommerce teams on unpublished retainers. TUXEN&Co sells an "eCommerce Manager" subscription at $2,495/mo, but that's request execution only. Fractional ecommerce directors cost $3K-15K/mo (US), and fractional CMOs A$10K-18K/mo (Australia). The middle (strategy plus execution plus AI, from $1,500/mo) is empty.
- **The top reason brands leave agencies is delivery dissatisfaction (48%), then "didn't understand our business"** (SetUp marketing relationship survey). A named senior lead who owns the whole problem answers both.

**Offshore objections and answers**
- **"Cheap means low quality"** (AU founders): price on profit after ads, report from Shopify and Xero rather than the ad platforms, offer a paid 30-day pilot, then month to month after the first term.
- **"Time zones slow sprints"** (AU managers): Sydney lunchtime calls (6:30-7:30 AM IST), plus overnight QA as an advantage.
- **"Offshore means juniors"** (US): name the senior leads; calls in US mornings (India evening).
- **"IP and GDPR risk"** (UK): a UK GDPR data processing agreement with Standard Contractual Clauses, code ownership in the contract, a UK-hours project lead.
- **"Every agency is in India"** (IN): report profit after RTO, not ROAS.

**Buying style by region** (general guidance, not tax advice)
- **Australia:** relationship-led and reference-driven. Payment terms about 30-36 days. Invoice in AUD; imported B2B services are generally outside GST for GST-registered buyers.
- **US:** fast and ROI-led, with MSA plus SOW and net 30. No federal VAT.
- **UK:** more formal procurement (RFP, security and GDPR checks). Reverse charge VAT. Net 30.
- **India:** most price-sensitive; expect to negotiate. 18% GST on domestic invoices; export invoices can be zero-rated under a Letter of Undertaking (LUT).

## 4. The offer: Marginwise Desk (single point of contact)

**How it works for the client**
- **One named lead:** Abhimanyu. Velmurgan joins for paid media.
- **One shared channel** (Slack, or WhatsApp in India), one request board, one weekly scorecard covering contribution profit, MER, conversion rate and AI visibility.
- **Anything about revenue or margin goes to the desk:**
  - We do it ourselves, or bring in a vetted partner we manage.
  - **The client never manages vendors.**
- **Terms:** a 90-day first term, then month to month, with pausing allowed. A flat monthly fee; no hour counting.

| Tier | Who it suits | Included | Response times |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Desk** | P1, P7, smaller brands | Named lead, 1 active request at a time (dev, CRO, SEO or ops), monthly profit review, vendor coordination | Reply in 1 business day; most requests done in 72 hours |
| **Operator** | P1, P2, P5 | Desk plus paid media management, 2 CRO tests a month, 2 active requests, weekly 30-minute call, monthly business review | Same-day reply in your time zone; urgent fixes within 4 hours |
| **Partner** | P2, P3, P5, P6 | Operator plus SEO and AI search programme, one AI agent run, management of your other vendors, quarterly roadmap and review | Urgent fixes within 2 hours; quarterly business review |

AI agents (support, sync, reconciliation, phone) are added to any tier as a setup fee plus a run fee. Every agent ships with a stated automation target.

**How two people deliver it**
1. Abhimanyu is the only client-facing lead and owns strategy, QA and CRO.
2. Velmurgan runs paid media.
3. White-label partners handle overflow Shopify dev, design, and links/PR.
4. AI agents run on proven engines (Retell, Trillet or n8n for voice and workflows; Gorgias for support).
5. Cap the book at 8-10 Desk or Operator clients. Hire or partner before going past the cap, not after.

## 6. Currency on every page (website rules)
1. **Detect the country on the server.** Use the hosting provider's country header (Cloudflare `cf-ipcountry`) first, then the browser time zone, then a saved choice. The `?market=` link overrides all of them. This shows the right currency on first paint, with no flash of USD.
2. **Map countries to currencies:**
   - AU to AUD; US to USD; GB to GBP; IN to INR.
   - NZ to NZD; CA to CAD; eurozone and Ireland to EUR; AE to AED; SG to SGD.
   - Everything else to USD.
3. **Apply it to every price and money value on the site:**
   - the home page plans
   - pricing
   - every service and AI solution page
   - market pages
   - calculator defaults
   - the revenue bands in the lead form
   - the free store check
   - booking and lead confirmation emails, using the lead's currency
   - the team Leads tab, which shows both the lead's currency and USD
4. **Currency switcher.** It sits in the header, is remembered, and lists the 4 core currencies plus "Other (converted)".
5. **One source of truth.** A single pricing config (`src/lib/pricing.ts`) holds the core price table and the FX snapshot. No page hard-codes a price.
6. **Machine-readable copies.**
   - **JSON-LD:** keep USD offers and add an `Offer` per core currency.
   - **llms.txt:** lists all four price tables.
7. **Tests.** Every page renders the right currency for AU, US, UK, IN and NZ; the rounding rules work; the FX snapshot date is shown.

## 7. Lead plan

**What the evidence says**
- **Referrals.** They are "by far" agencies' biggest source of new business, and 15% name partner referrals first (SparkToro 2025).
- **Cold email.** Benchmarks run from 0.45% (Belkins) to 5-9% (Instantly), both vendor claims. Plan on 1-3% replies.
- **Shopify Partner Directory.** Out of reach for now: Plus tier needs US$500K referral revenue. Register as a partner anyway and log every store, because it counts later.

**90-day plan, Australia first**
- **Weeks 1-2: set up.**
  1. Register as a Shopify Partner. Join Klaviyo, Gorgias and Recharge. Create a Clutch profile.
  2. Build a list of 150 Australian Shopify stores at A$1-20M that still show surcharge text or checkout friction.
  3. Set up a separate sending domain with SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
  4. Make a Loom teardown template.
- **Weeks 3-6, each week:**
  1. 60 trigger emails, sent only to published role addresses (see compliance).
  2. 25 LinkedIn invites and 2 posts.
  3. 3 helpful answers in communities.
  4. Contact 3 accountant partners, offering a free margin check for their clients.
  - **Target by week 6:** 5+ replies, 3 teardowns sent, 2 calls, 1 signed partner.
- **Weeks 7-10:**
  1. Same weekly volume.
  2. Pitch Add To Cart with the surcharge data story.
  3. One co-marketing teardown with a Shopify app developer.
  4. Ask the first client for a Clutch review.
  - **Target, cumulative:** 6 calls, 2 proposals, 1 paid audit or project.
- **Weeks 11-13:**
  1. Turn results into the first real case file (with written permission).
  2. Start the US de minimis sequence at 30 emails a week.
  3. Keep the channel that produced calls; cut the rest.
  - **Target by day 90:** 8-10 calls, 3 proposals, 1-2 clients, 2 active referral partners.

These targets are planning assumptions, not benchmarks.

**Outbound compliance** (check against each law before sending)
- **Australia:** Spam Act. Email only conspicuously published business addresses, about the recipient's role. Identify the sender (name and ABN or legal name). Honour unsubscribes within 5 working days. ACMA fined Lululemon A$702K over unsubscribes.
- **UK:** PECR allows B2B email to limited companies and LLPs with identification and an opt-out. Sole traders need consent. Document a legitimate-interests assessment for named contacts.
- **US:** CAN-SPAM. Accurate headers, a postal address, opt-out honoured within 10 business days.
- **India:** DPDP Rules notified Nov 2025, with core duties from about May 2027. Keep consent records now.

## 9. What could go wrong
- **Scope creep inside Desk.** "One request at a time" plus a written scope of what counts as a request. Anything larger becomes a sprint.
- **Capacity.** Two people with a day job. Hard cap of 8-10 clients; partners vetted before the first overflow job.
- **Thin proof.** Every claim stays sourced. The first two clients get a discount in exchange for a named case file and a Clutch review.
- **Compliance in outbound.** Follow the rules above; one complaint in Australia costs more than a quarter of leads.
- **India price pressure.** Keep India to performance marketing plus RTO, where we have a clear angle. Don't sell Partner-tier depth at India prices.
