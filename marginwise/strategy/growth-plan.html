Marginwise strategy, 28 Oct 2026

One desk for every store problem

Global growth plan: the single-point-of-contact offer, regional prices in local currency, buyer personas, the lead plan and the website currency rules.

Based on 59 competitor agencies (AU, UK, US, India), a packaging benchmark of 25+ agencies, lead-channel evidence and 7 personas. Competitor prices and results are their own claims.

The decision

Become the one desk a growing Shopify brand calls for anything that touches revenue or margin: ads, conversion, the store and its integrations, search and AI visibility, and the operations AI can take over. Sell it as Marginwise Desk, a monthly plan with a named lead, one channel and one request board, at published local prices. Use small fixed-price entry offers to start. Win the first clients through referral partners and trigger-based outreach in Australia. Report monthly on contribution profit, not ROAS.

Why the position is open

Nobody publishes prices

Only 4 of 59 agencies show any price. None of the 10 Australian agencies reviewed does.

Nobody sells AI for operations

Competitor "AI" means creative or reporting. No one packages order-status, ERP sync, payout reconciliation or phone agents.

The middle is empty

Fractional ecommerce directors cost $3K-15K/mo (US), fractional CMOs A$10K-18K/mo (AU). TUXEN&Co's $2,495/mo subscription is execution only. Strategy plus execution plus AI from $1,500/mo has no owner.

Why brands leave agencies

Delivery dissatisfaction (48%), then "didn't understand our business" (SetUp survey). A named senior lead who owns the whole problem answers both.

Currency on every page

  1. Detect the country on the server: Cloudflare cf-ipcountry, then browser time zone, then saved choice. ?market= overrides all. Right currency on first paint, no flash of USD.
  2. Map country to currency: AU-AUD, US-USD, GB-GBP, IN-INR, NZ-NZD, CA-CAD, eurozone and IE-EUR, AE-AED, SG-SGD, everything else USD.
  3. Apply to every money value: home plans, pricing, service and AI solution pages, market pages, calculator defaults, lead form revenue bands, store check, confirmation emails, and the team Leads tab (lead's currency plus USD).
  4. Header currency switcher, remembered, with the 4 core currencies plus "Other (converted)".
  5. One source of truth: src/lib/pricing.ts holds the price table and FX snapshot. No page hard-codes a price.
  6. Machine-readable: JSON-LD with an Offer per core currency; llms.txt lists all four price tables.
  7. Tests: correct currency for AU, US, UK, IN and NZ; rounding rules; FX snapshot date shown.

Outbound compliance

  • Australia, Spam Act: email only conspicuously published business addresses, about the recipient's role. Identify the sender (name and ABN). Honour unsubscribes within 5 working days. ACMA fined Lululemon A$702K over unsubscribes.
  • UK, PECR: B2B email to limited companies and LLPs with identification and opt-out. Sole traders need consent. Keep a legitimate-interests assessment.
  • US, CAN-SPAM: accurate headers, postal address, opt-out within 10 business days.
  • India, DPDP: Rules notified Nov 2025, core duties from about May 2027. Keep consent records now.

What could go wrong

Scope creep inside Desk

One request at a time, with a written definition of a request. Anything larger becomes a sprint.

Capacity

Two people with a day job. Hard cap of 8-10 clients; partners vetted before the first overflow job.

Thin proof

Every claim stays sourced. First two clients get a discount for a named case file and a Clutch review.

Outbound compliance

One complaint in Australia costs more than a quarter of leads. Follow the rules above.

India price pressure

Keep India to performance marketing plus RTO. Don't sell Partner-tier depth at India prices.
