10 AI builds worth starting with for ecommerce brands

AI is most useful where your team repeats the same work every week. Each build below lists what it does, what data it needs, and what to watch. Start with one, measure it for a month, then add the next.

How we use AI ourselves: to read every review and ticket, draft test ideas and first drafts, and summarise data. A person who has built D2C products decides what to fix first and checks everything before it ships.

Customer service

  • Support assistant for the top questions. Answers 'where is my order', returns and sizing from your own policies and order data; hands over to a person when unsure. Watch: wrong answers on refunds, so keep a person in the loop.
  • Reply drafting for your team. Drafts answers to tickets in your brand voice for an agent to approve. Watch: tone and promises.
  • Return reason sorting. Groups return and review comments into reasons (size, quality, delivery) so you fix the product page or the product.

Content and SEO

  • Product description and alt text drafts. Writes first drafts from spec sheets; a person checks every claim. Watch: identical, generic copy.
  • Review summaries for product pages. Turns hundreds of reviews into the top pros and objections, so copy answers what buyers worry about.
  • FAQ and comparison pages. Drafts answers to the questions customers actually ask, for search and AI answers.

Ads and growth

  • Ad copy and hook variations. Generates many hooks from one brief so you can test more angles. Watch: claims that breach ad policies.
  • Weekly performance summary. Pulls Shopify, GA4 and ad platform numbers into a plain-English summary every Monday, with the three things that changed.

Operations

  • Stock and demand alerts. Flags best sellers about to run out and slow movers to promote.
  • Personalised post-purchase emails. Recommends the next product from what the customer bought, inside your email tool.

Before you build anything

  • Pick one task your team repeats weekly and measure how long it takes today
  • Decide what a person must always check before the AI output reaches a customer
  • Keep customer data inside tools you already trust, and check each tool's data terms
  • Measure the result after 30 days: hours saved, tickets resolved, conversion or revenue change
