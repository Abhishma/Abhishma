## What everyone does (so it doesn't differentiate us)
1. Free audit or call as the only way in, with prices hidden. Only AEO Engine, BeingEcom, XgenTech and Sqroot publish any price.
2. Proof by volume: logo walls, "spend managed", ROAS multiples.
3. "Senior team" or "one team under one roof".
4. Partner badges and Clutch or Google review counts.
5. AI as a label: creative, reporting, GEO, or "agentic readiness".

## Gaps nobody owns, and how Marginwise takes them
1. **Published fixed prices, including a paid audit.** None of the 10 Australian agencies shows a fee. Marginwise shows four price columns. Keep prices visible on every service page.
2. **AI that runs operations, not just ads.** No competitor sells a support agent, ERP sync agent, payout reconciliation or AI receptionist as a product. Lead with it.
3. **Profit after ads for the mid-market.** Optamize and Elephant Room want A$5M+ or big brands; Top Growth Marketing and BeingEcom are the only others using margin language. Say plainly: "Built for growing Shopify brands, about A$2M to A$50M a year."
4. **Ads, CRO, build and AI in one fixed-scope sprint.** Specialists do one channel; full-service agencies are enterprise or sell on ROAS.
5. **Proof you can check.** Competitors show unsourced totals and even stock headshots. Every Marginwise number has a source, and client results will be shown as contribution profit with permission.
6. **Honest offshore trust.** No offshore agency publishes overlap hours or local-currency prices. Marginwise names the hours, the lead, the weekly update day, NDA and IP terms, and local prices.

## Risks to handle
- **Name clash:**
  - Optamize's free "Profit Leak Audit" (recorded teardown plus action plan) is close to our free teardown and "Revenue Leak Audit".
  - Blend and BeingEcom also use "leak" language.
  - Recommendation: rename the paid audit "Margin Audit", which matches the brand and is distinct. Decision for Abhimanyu.
- **"Australian managed" pitch.** Digital Choice sells against offshore teams. Answer openly on the site: why an India-based team, overlap hours, who you speak to, contracts and data handling.
- **Superlatives.** Don't copy "#1", "leading" or "best": ASCI, the Australian Consumer Law and the UK CAP Code all require proof.
