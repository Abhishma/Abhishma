# Reference review: Avenue and ChatSEO (23 Oct 2026)

Sources: live pages, onlyavenue.com and chatseo.app, read 23 Oct 2026. The prices and claims below are the vendors' own; we have not verified them. The Instagram reference post (instagram.com/p/DeGobR1gWaZ) couldn't be read, because Instagram blocks automated access.

## Avenue (onlyavenue.com)

What it is: Australian booking software for service businesses (mechanics, electricians, joiners, equipment hire). It has:
- a "Book online" button on the Google Business Profile (Reserve with Google)
- branded booking forms with SMS and email reminders
- automatic review requests
- a floating contact button for the website
- a 24/7 AI call agent called "Sophie"

Prices shown (site is en-AU): Standard $199/mo, Premium $395/mo, AI call agent $399/mo, Enterprise on request.

Fit for Marginwise:
- **As a tool for us: no.** It serves trades and service bookings, not ecommerce.
- **As a competitor benchmark: yes.** Our AI receptionist (in the solution kit and the calculator) targets the same Australian trades. Avenue sets the local price anchor: about A$400/mo for an AI call agent. If we sell to trades, we price against that, or partner and refer instead of building.
- **Website tactics worth copying:**
  - "Type your business name, we'll build the rest". The visitor gets value instantly, before any call.
  - A live demo with no sign-up ("Call Sophie").
  - Before-and-after numbers from named clients.

## ChatSEO (chatseo.app)

What it is: an AI SEO agent from €29/mo. It connects Google Search Console and DataForSEO data, audits a site, and returns "the 3 actions that move the needle", ranked by traffic at stake. It can publish fixes to WordPress, Shopify and Webflow, and plugs into Claude and ChatGPT through MCP and an API.

Fit for Marginwise:
- **As a tool for us: worth a trial.** marginwise.in had 2 clicks and 3 impressions in Google from 22 Sep to 19 Oct. A €29/mo agent that reads our Search Console data costs less than Semrush. It also suits client SEO retainers.
- **Watch-outs:**
  - Review every recommendation before applying it.
  - Turn off auto-publish to client stores.
  - Get a data processing agreement before connecting client Search Console accounts.
  - Most reviews come from French users.
- **Website tactics worth copying:**
  - A URL-input hero ("Enter your website").
  - Output limited to 3 ranked actions.
  - A cost-comparison table against the tools it replaces.
  - Testimonials shown as Search Console screenshots.

## What Marginwise should do with this

1. Make the free teardown instant. Add a store URL field in the hero ("Enter your store URL"). It runs our leak-audit crawler and shows a score plus the 3 biggest fixes on screen, then offers the 2-minute video and the call. This is the strongest pattern on both sites, and we already have the crawler.
2. Use the "3 actions that move the needle" format in teardown videos, emails and Instagram.
3. Trial ChatSEO for 30 days on marginwise.in. Track Search Console clicks and impressions before and after.
4. Use Avenue's prices as the benchmark for any AI receptionist offer to Australian trades.
5. Once real clients agree, show results as screenshots with names (Search Console or Shopify). Until then, keep "Example case. Not a client."
