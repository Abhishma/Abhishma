# Competitor review: Adscrey (adscrey.com, @adscrey), 24 Oct 2026

Sources: the live website, and the Instagram profile and latest 12 reels (via vidIQ). Adscrey's figures are their own claims and are unverified.

## Who they are
- An Indian D2C growth agency (Gurugram, with offices in Bengaluru and Dubai). Founded 2021. Founder @d2cbyshivam.
- Claims: "India's #1 Performance Marketing Agency", 50+ team, 450+ businesses scaled, ₹300+ crore client revenue, 5+ average ROAS, ₹60+ crore ad spend.
- Services: Meta and Google ads, Shopify builds, social media management, SEO, marketplace management (Nykaa, Flipkart, Myntra), ad creatives and UGC, scaling strategy, CRO and landing pages.
- Clients: mostly Indian fashion, jewellery, ethnic wear, home decor, beauty and supplement brands on Shopify.
- For Marginwise: **a direct competitor in India**, not in Australia, the US or the UK.

## Website: what works
1. A logo wall with filters by category (Fashion, Jewellery, Beauty and so on). A visitor finds brands like theirs in one click.
2. A numbers bar near the top. It's simple social proof, read in 3 seconds.
3. A separate page for each service (/pages/performance-marketing, /pages/seo). Good for SEO; Marginwise has only sections on one page.
4. Policy pages: privacy, terms, service delivery and refund. Payment gateways like Razorpay and Stripe in India expect refund and delivery policies.
5. Named brand case cards, and a visual of the engagement steps (discovery call, onboarding, regular catch-up, delivery).
6. Office addresses, Instagram, LinkedIn, a blog and a newsletter.

## Website: what to avoid
- "India's #1" and other superlatives with no source. India's advertising code (ASCI), the Australian Consumer Law and UK CAP rules all expect claims like this to be substantiated.
- "5+ average ROAS" as the headline result. ROAS isn't profit. This is exactly where Marginwise is different.
- Generic FAQ written for keywords ("What is growth marketing?"), with weak grammar. It adds no value and doesn't help rankings under Google's helpful-content guidance.
- The viewport blocks zooming (user-scalable=no), which is an accessibility failure. 120+ logo images slow the page.

Takeaways:
- Testimonials from real client founders beat every other format by 5-50x. They're also what they put ad budget behind.
- Educational tips get steady but small reach.
- Office humour gets views from non-buyers and almost no engagement. Skip it.
- 36K followers but about 3 likes per reel: follower count is a vanity number. Judge content by saves, shares, DMs and booked calls.

## What Marginwise should take from this
1. **Client founder testimonial videos.** After the first 2-3 clients, record a 30-60 second founder video: the problem, what changed, a number they agree to share. Pin it and run it as an ad. Until then, keep "Example case. Not a client."
2. **Founder-to-camera tips with a screen visual.** One problem, one fix, under 45 seconds, Abhimanyu on camera. Topics: "One Shopify checkout setting that costs Australian stores money", "Why ROAS 3 can still lose money". Pair with the case-file carousels.
3. **Website:**
   - a separate page for each of the 5 services
   - pages for the categories you serve (fashion, jewellery, beauty, home decor, supplements), in each market
   - refund and service delivery policies, alongside the privacy and terms pages already queued
   - a numbers bar using only true numbers (for example: 4 markets, fixed published prices, teardown within 3 working days)
   - a category-filtered logo wall once there are clients
4. **Positioning against Adscrey in India.** Don't compete on size (50+ team, 450 brands). Compete on:
   - profit after ads, not ROAS
   - fixed, published prices
   - AI tools that agencies like this don't offer (sync agents, support agents, the leak-audit tool)
5. **Possible new service for India: marketplace management** (Nykaa, Myntra, Flipkart, Amazon). Indian D2C brands buy it with ads. Add it only if Velmurgan's team can deliver it; otherwise partner and refer.
