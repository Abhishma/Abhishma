The verdict

No single culprit. Three small leaks, working together.

  1. Show delivery cost and date on the product page. No surprise at checkout.
  2. Remove the surcharge app. Then reprice the thin-margin products.
  3. Cut the apps and compress the images. Aim for under 2.5 s on a phone.

Fix them in that order: biggest leak first.
