Evidence A · Performance marketing

Ads Manager says ROAS 3. The bank says A$80.

EXHIBIT A
ROAS (Ads Manager)3.0

Clue: A$10,000 of ads. Ads Manager counts A$30,000 of sales; we assumed 20% of that would have happened anyway, leaving A$24,000. That's 240 orders at A$100. After product cost, fees, returns and shipping, each order leaves A$42 to pay for the ads. Profit: A$80.

The verdict

Four suspects. One plan, in this order.

  1. Performance marketing: track profit after ads, not ROAS. Cut what loses money before you scale.
  2. CRO: put Add to cart, Afterpay and the delivery date on the first screen.
  3. AI tools: an assistant answers "where is my order?" from Shopify and hands the rest to a person.
  4. Web and app development: a wholesale portal where stockists order directly, with live stock.
