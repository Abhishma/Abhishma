## Slides

All numbers on slides 4 to 6 are an example store, worked through the Marginwise surcharge calculator. Put "Example store" on each of those slides.

**Slide 1: hook**
- Headline: The surcharge is gone. The card fee isn't.
- Sub: Since 1 October, who pays it is up to you.
- Visual: a checkout summary line "Card surcharge 1.5%  A$1.50" with a strike through it.

**Slide 2: what changed**
- Headline: What changed on 1 October
- Body: Australian businesses can no longer add a surcharge to Visa, Mastercard or eftpos card payments. The RBA also cut the interchange fees banks charge on those cards.
- Footer: Source: Reserve Bank of Australia, media release 2026-10

**Slide 3: where stores still leak**
- Headline: Check these 4 places first
- Body:
  1. Theme or cart text that still says "card fee"
  2. A payment or fee app still adding the line
  3. Invoices, quotes and B2B order forms
  4. FAQs and the shipping and payment pages
- Footer: Removing the line is the easy part.

**Slide 4: the margin hit (example store)**
- Headline: What the ban costs a store this size
- Body: A$100,000 a month in card sales. It used to add a 1.5% surcharge.
- Big number: A$1,500 a month it no longer collects
- Line under it: A$18,000 a year
- Label: Example store, Marginwise surcharge calculator

**Slide 5: option 1, absorb or reprice**
- Headline: Option 1: absorb it, or reprice
- Body:
  - Absorb: the A$18,000 comes straight out of margin.
  - Reprice: to keep the same net after fees, prices need to rise by the old surcharge rate, 1.5% here.
  - Do it by product. Raise prices on lines with room; leave your traffic drivers alone.
- Label: Example store

**Slide 6: option 2, steer to cheaper ways to pay**
- Headline: Option 2: steer to cheaper ways to pay
- Body: If 20% of card sales move to a method that costs 1 point less (1.6% down to 0.6% in this example), you save A$200 a month.
- Line under it: Helpful, but not a full fix. Most stores need some of option 1 as well.
- Label: Example store. Ask your payment provider for your actual rates.

**Slide 7: what to do this week**
- Headline: This week
- Body:
  1. Search the theme, apps and invoices for "surcharge" and "card fee"
  2. Pull last month's card sales and fee rate from your payment reports
  3. Put your own numbers into the calculator
  4. Reprice by product, not across the board
- Footer: Your accountant or adviser confirms what applies to you.

## Risks and how this post avoids them

- **Legal advice:**
  - The post only says what the RBA decision covers and points people to their adviser.
  - It doesn't say whether Amex, PayPal or BNPL can still be surcharged; tell people to check with their provider.
- **Invented results:** every number is labelled as an example store, worked through our tested calculator. There is no client name and no client result.
- **Rates vary:**
  - The 1.6% and 0.6% fee rates are illustrative. Slide 6 says so.
  - If anyone asks about real rates, send them to their own payment reports.
- **Dead CTA:** PROFIT already has a working auto-DM. Don't swap in a new keyword like SURCHARGE until its flow is built and tested.
