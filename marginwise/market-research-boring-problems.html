Marginwise market research: boring problems we can solve

Australia, New Zealand (Auckland), US, Canada, Germany (Berlin), Saudi Arabia. Research date 17 Oct 2026.

How to read this: "boring problems" are operational, recurring and often compliance-driven. Brands must fix them whether or not the economy is good, which makes them easier to sell than "growth". Every statistic has a source in section 12. Anything marked (unverified) has not been confirmed against a primary source and must not go into client-facing material until it is. Deal sizes and forecasts are our planning estimates, not market data.

Planning exchange rates used for USD pricing (check before quoting): 1 AUD = 0.66 USD, 1 NZD = 0.59 USD, 1 CAD = 0.72 USD, 1 EUR = 1.10 USD, 1 USD = 3.75 SAR (pegged).

0. The decision in ten lines

  1. Start with Australia, and run the US alongside it. Both are English-speaking, the Instagram account already targets them, and each has a live trigger right now: the Australian card surcharge ban (1 Oct 2026) and US tariff repricing.
  2. Add New Zealand at almost no extra cost. Sell to it through Xero bookkeepers and trans-Tasman brands, using the same AU content.
  3. Start Germany (Berlin) in month 2, aimed at English-speaking startups. Lead with B2B e-invoicing (mandatory from 1 Jan 2027) and the withdrawal-button and accessibility rules. Before you start, line up a German-speaking reviewer and a law-firm partner.
  4. Start Canada in month 2 as an extension of the US work. Lead with cross-border duties and Quebec French requirements.
  5. Start Saudi Arabia in month 3, through white-label partners only. Lead with ZATCA Wave 25 e-invoicing (deadline 1 Feb 2027). Do not sell brand marketing there yet.
  6. Lead with fixed-fee entry offers ($1,500 to $8,000). Then convert clients into retainers: performance marketing, CRO, and AI agent monitoring. The retainers are where the money compounds.
  7. The edge to sell: integration plus AI at India cost. Most local agencies do creative work or platform work. Few do Shopify plus ERP plus Salesforce plus compliance in a single fixed scope.
  8. Price at about 40-60% below local agencies. Sell the outcome, not the hourly rate. Buyers read a very low price as risk.
  9. 90-day booked revenue forecast across all markets: $11.5K conservative, $72K base, $162K stretch (section 7). The base case assumes about 1,500 targeted contacts and an average deal size of $5-8K.
  10. Biggest risk: delivery capacity and trust. Fix both with a named founder on calls, a fixed-scope contract, and one case study from a demo store before day 21.

11.5 Testing and kill rules (our heuristics)

  • Kill an ad when either is true:
  • it has spent 2x the target cost per lead with zero leads, or
  • its 3-second hook rate is below 20% after 1,000 impressions.
  • Kill a concept after 7 days if its cost per DM keyword is more than 2x the account median.
  • Scale a winner by 20-30% every 3-4 days, and copy it into retargeting.
  • Frequency:
  • Cold audiences: under 2.5 a week.
  • Retargeting: 4-6 a week while the audience is small.
  • Refresh creative when CTR falls 30% from its peak.
  • Metrics, in order:
  • Hook rate.
  • Cost per DM keyword.
  • DM-to-email capture.
  • Cost per lead.
  • Cost per booked call.
  • Show rate.
  • Audit close rate.
  • CAC per paid audit.

  1. Many small interest or lookalike ad sets on $50 a day. Consolidate instead.
  2. Near-duplicate creatives. Andromeda treats them as one ad.
  3. Optimising for cheap DMs instead of booked calls. Send CRM events back.
  4. IG DM ads into Germany, missing DSA fields, or over-promising claims.
  5. Launching all six markets at once, so each gets $8 a day and none learns anything.
