Where ecommerce revenue leaks

        
Most stores lose revenue in the same places. These are the published benchmarks we audit against, and what we do about each one.

        

          

            
70.22%of carts

            
are abandoned on average, across 50 studies of online shopping behaviour.

            
What we do: map every step from cart to payment and remove the reasons shoppers leave, starting with surprise costs and forced account creation.

            Source: Baymard Institute, cart abandonment rate
          

          

            
35%higher conversion

            
is what the average large ecommerce site can gain through better checkout design alone.

            
What we do: score your checkout against usability guidelines, then test the highest-impact fixes first.

            Source: Baymard Institute, checkout usability research
          

          

            
8.4%more conversions

            
for retail sites from a 0.1-second improvement in mobile site speed, in a study of 37 brands.

            
What we do: find the apps, scripts and images slowing your store, and fix speed before spending more on traffic.

            Source: Deloitte Digital and Google, Milliseconds Make Millions (2020), via The Drum
          

          

            
1 Oct2026

            
is when Australian merchants had to stop surcharging Visa, Mastercard and eftpos card payments.

            
What we do: remove surcharge logic from the theme, apps and checkout, then show your margin by product after card fees so you can absorb, reprice or steer customers to cheaper ways to pay.

            Source: Reserve Bank of Australia, media release 2026-10

Questions

        
Is the call free?
Yes. It is a 30-minute conversation. If there is a fit, you get a written scope and quote afterwards, in USD, AUD or GBP.

        
How do I get the meeting invite?
Pick a time and save the Google Calendar invite. We usually confirm within the hour and send a meeting link by email.

        
Who will I actually speak to?
Abhimanyu leads the first call. If paid media is part of the brief, Velmurgan joins too. If you go ahead, you meet the specialists on your account, and Abhimanyu stays your point of contact.

        
How much does it cost?
The 2-minute video teardown is free. The Revenue Leak Audit is $1,500. Fixed-price sprints start at $3,900 and monthly retainers at $1,500. Larger builds and AI agents are quoted after the first call, with a written proposal.

        
Can we speak to a past client?
Yes. After the first call, we can connect you with a client from a similar category or market, with their permission.

        
Will you sign an NDA?
Yes, if you need one before sharing details. Mention it in the brief and send it with the confirmation.

        
Do you work with Australian, UK and US brands from India?
Yes. Sydney afternoons, the London working day and US mornings all overlap with our hours, so calls happen in your daytime and work moves on overnight. The picker shows each slot in your time zone and ours.

        
Do you use AI on client work?
Yes, for first-pass audits, drafts and the agents we build. A person reviews every finding and approves anything that touches money, prices or orders. Client data only goes into tools covered by a data processing agreement.

        
Can you help with rules like the surcharge ban or accessibility?
We implement the changes: checkout, theme, tracking and invoices. Your lawyer or adviser confirms what applies to you. We do not give legal advice.

        
Can we hire you for one service only?
Yes. Many brands start with a CRO audit or an ads account review, then add design or development once the first results are in.

        
What should I prepare?
Your store URL, rough monthly traffic and ad spend, and the problem in a sentence. Read-only access to GA4 or your ads account helps.
