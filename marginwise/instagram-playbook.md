| @thrivelab.in | 3.2K | "100+ D2C brands", founder handle linked | Nothing breaks out; best is a 3-point checklist at 5.3K | Hinglish memes about agency life: 1.6-4K plays, under 40 likes |
| @smashed.agency | 1.8K | Names clients (Frido, Oziva, Canva, Slice, Phool) | Factory-floor shoot BTS: 877 | Generic founder talking-head advice ("10L vs 1Cr", "first 30 days with us"): 300-500 plays |

### What this tells us

1. **Proof is the hook.** The reels that travel show numbers: dashboard screenshots, month-end results, a client's journey, a founder testimonial. Advice with no proof behind it stalls (Smashed).
2. **Saveable resources earn the most engagement.** Brandslane's "save these ad libraries" reel had the highest like rate in the set. Our teardowns and lead magnets fit this.
3. **Memes, office culture and holiday posts do not work** for winning clients (Thrive Labs, Adscrey). Skip them.
4. **Bios lead with a proof number.** Every account has one. Marginwise has no client numbers it can publish yet; this is the biggest gap.
5. **Founder testimonials get pinned.** Adscrey's top two pinned reels are client founders on camera.
6. **Founder plus agency account split** is common (@d2cbyshivam, @gaurav_speaks_). This supports the Collab-post approach.
7. **Nobody talks about margin or conversion.** All four sell ROAS and revenue ("10x+ ROAS", "₹500 Cr+"). Margin remains open ground.
8. **Discount the big follower counts.** Adscrey's recent reels get 2-5K plays and about 3 likes each, which suggests most reach is paid. Do not benchmark against it.

### What we do not copy

- Headline ROAS or revenue claims without context. They contradict our own agency checklist ("ask for a client you can call") and our margin message.
- Showing client dashboards or naming clients without written permission.

## 4. Proof plan (new priority)

Without proof, reach will look like Smashed's. Build it in this order:

1. **Teardown proof (week 1 onward):** every teardown shows skill without needing a client.
2. **Before/after on your own work:** with permission, a past fix with the metric it moved. If no permission, describe the pattern without the brand.
3. **First testimonial (target: within 60 days):** offer 2-3 brands the Growth Audit at a reduced fee in exchange for a written or voice testimonial (their face optional) and permission to show the metric that moved.
4. **Monthly "what we fixed" roundup (once 2+ clients):** Brandslane's results format, but with margin and conversion, not ROAS.

Do not use results from current or past employers. Check the employment-contract point before posting anything.
